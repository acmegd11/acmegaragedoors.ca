Common Opener Issues We Repair Daily in Niagara

Stripped Drive Gears

When an opener motor hums or spins rapidly without lifting the chain or belt, the internal white nylon helical drive gear has stripped. We carry replacement OEM gear kits for all LiftMaster and Chamberlain models, saving you hundreds over replacing the entire motor.

Blown Logic Boards & Capacitors

Ontario summer thunderstorms and winter power surges can fry the circuit board inside older openers. We diagnose voltage issues, replace starting capacitors, and replace damaged receiver boards.

Misaligned Safety Photo-Eyes

If your door starts closing, then stops and reverses with the opener light clicking or flashing 10 times, the safety beams are out of alignment. We realign, secure brackets, and test sensitivity.

Remote Range & Keypad Failure

Having to pull your bumper up to the door before your remote responds? We test radio frequency interference, install extended-range antennae, and program wireless keypads.
