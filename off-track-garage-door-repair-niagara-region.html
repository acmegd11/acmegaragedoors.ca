⚠️ WARNING: Never Force an Off-Track Door

When rollers pop out of the track, the remaining hinges and single remaining cable bear the entire weight of the door at an unnatural angle. Attempting to force the door closed, kicking the track, or pulling the emergency release cord can cause the entire horizontal ceiling track to rip free from the ceiling framing, sending the door crashing down.

Common Causes of an Off-Track Garage Door

  • Vehicle or Bumper Collision: Accidental contact while backing out bends vertical tracks and pops lower rollers.
  • Snapped or Slack Counterbalance Cable: If one cable snaps, the door twists instantly and jams sideways in the track.
  • Seized Roller Bearings: Cheap steel rollers seize up, drag against the metal track, and jump the track lip.
  • Obstruction in the Track Path: Brooms, garbage cans, or ice buildup in the lower vertical track wedge the door rollers out of alignment.

Our Professional Realignment Process

  1. Locking Down the Door: We secure the door using heavy-duty C-clamps and safety locking pliers to eliminate collapse risk.
  2. Relieving Spring Tension: Technicians safely balance the torsion springs before handling detached cables.
  3. Straightening & Realigning Tracks: We reform bent tracks, torque jamb brackets, and ensure 1/2-inch uniform clearance.
  4. Upgrading Rollers: We inspect and replace worn rollers with heavy-duty 13-ball sealed nylon rollers for whisper-quiet travel.
  5. Dynamic Balance & Safety Testing: We verify perfect level travel and recalibrate opener travel limit switches.
