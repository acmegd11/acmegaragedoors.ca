Why Homeowners Choose Acme for Garage Door Repair in Niagara Falls

A malfunctioning garage door is more than an inconvenience — it is a serious home security risk and a critical safety hazard. Whether your car is locked inside before your morning commute, a spring snapped with a loud boom, or the door is dangling crooked off the tracks, Acme Garage Doors is equipped to resolve the issue on our very first visit.

Common Garage Door Problems We Fix Daily:

  • Snapped or Fatigued Springs: Torsion and extension spring replacements calibrated specifically to your door's exact weight.
  • Crooked or Off-Track Doors: Full track realignment, bent track straightening, and heavy-duty steel roller resetting.
  • Frayed or Detached Cables: Commercial 7x19 aircraft-grade steel cable installation and cable drum balancing.
  • Opener Malfunctions: Troubleshooting LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Craftsman, and Genie motors, stripped gears, and sensor faults.
  • Weatherstripping & Winter Drafts: Heavy-duty bottom astragal seals and vinyl jamb perimeter weather seals designed for Ontario freezes.
