Our Story

Why Niagara Homeowners Count on Acme

We know how frustrating it is when a garage door breaks down before your morning commute or late on a freezing winter night. We built Acme to be the garage door company we would want working on our own homes.

Solving Niagara's Unique Garage Door Challenges

Living in the Niagara Peninsula between Lake Ontario and Lake Erie means our homes endure heavy snow, extreme freeze-thaw cycles, and corrosive winter road salt. Standard builder-grade hardware simply isn't built to survive here.

That is why Acme exclusively uses high-cycle oil-tempered torsion springs rated for 15,000+ cycles ($250–$350 including installation and 1 Year Spring Warranty), heavy-duty 7x19 aircraft-grade galvanized cables, and premium R-16.3 and R-18 Arctic polyurethane injected doors that stop freezing lakefront drafts from chilling the living spaces and bedrooms directly above your garage.

We don't cut corners with cheap parts or temporary fixes. When we repair or install your door, it is done right the first time.
