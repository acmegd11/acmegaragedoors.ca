Warning Signs Your Garage Door Cables Need Immediate Attention

  • Fraying Metal Strands: A cable is made of braided steel wires. If you notice even a few broken "whiskers" sticking out, the cable is on the verge of sudden failure.
  • Door Hanging Crooked: If one side of the door is 2 inches lower than the other when partially open, one cable has stretched or slipped off its spool.
  • Slack Cable Dangling: If a cable is loose and hanging like a limp rope while the door is down, your torsion spring tension has shifted or the cable drum set screws have slipped.
  • Severe Rust Near the Floor: In Ontario, road salt dripping off your car corrodes the bottom cable loops where they fasten to the bottom corner brackets.

Why We Always Install Heavy-Duty 7x19 Galvanized Cables

Standard builder cables use 7x7 strand construction that tends to kink and snap within 5 years. Acme exclusively installs 7x19 galvanized aircraft cables (19 micro-strands in each of the 7 bundles). This provides exceptional flexibility around cable drums, superior fatigue resistance, and higher breaking strength (over 1,800 lbs per cable).
