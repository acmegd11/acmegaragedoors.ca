Acme R-16 & R-18 Transformation (After)

  • Spring Life: 15,000-cycle high-tension oil-tempered springs calibrated for Canadian freeze cycles & backed by 1 Year Spring Warranty ($250–$350 including installation)
  • Noise & Vibration: 13-ball sealed nylon rollers & whisper-quiet belt-drive smart motor (dampens vibration by up to 28–32 dB)
  • Weather Protection: Commercial dual-fin Arctic EPDM rubber threshold and full perimeter vinyl jamb weatherstripping to prevent ice freeze-down
