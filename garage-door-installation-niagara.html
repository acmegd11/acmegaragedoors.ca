Why R-Value Insulation Matters in Ontario

An attached garage is the largest uninsulated opening in your building envelope. Cold winter winds from Lake Ontario and Lake Erie penetrate non-insulated doors, making bedrooms above the garage freezing cold.

Acme doors feature Neufoam™ continuous polyurethane insulation chemically bonded between heavy-gauge galvanized steel skins:

  • R-16.3 Thermal Performance: Blocks sub-zero drafts and keeps your garage 10°C to 15°C warmer throughout January and February.
  • Acoustic Noise Reduction: Polyurethane core absorbs exterior traffic and street noise by up to 28 decibels.
  • Superior Structural Rigidity: Sandwiched steel resists hockey puck impacts and high wind loads up to 130 km/h.
