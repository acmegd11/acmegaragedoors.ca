What's Included in Our 25-Point Maintenance Checklist?

Unlike quick superficial oiling, our certified technicians spend a full 45 to 60 minutes servicing every mechanical and electrical component of your garage door system:

Counterbalance & Springs

  • Spring balance test (neutral lift at waist height)
  • End bearing and center plate inspection
  • Shaft collar and set screw torque check
  • Silicone coating on spring coils to prevent friction

Tracks & Rollers

  • Plumb alignment of vertical and horizontal tracks
  • Torque lag bolts securing jamb brackets into studs
  • Inspect roller bearings for flat spots or wobbling
  • Clean track channels of road grit and debris

Cables & Drums

  • Micro-inspection for single broken cable strands
  • Inspect aluminum cable drum grooves for burrs
  • Synchronize left and right cable tension
  • Check bottom bracket cotter pins and fasteners

Safety & Weatherproofing

  • UL325 photo-eye beam alignment & optical test
  • Mechanical 2x4 auto-reverse safety test
  • Inspect bottom rubber astragal seal flexibility
  • Examine perimeter vinyl jamb weatherstripping

⚠️ Important Homeowner Tip: Why You Should NEVER Use WD-40

Standard WD-40 is a solvent and degreaser — not a lubricant! Spraying WD-40 on your garage door strips away factory grease, collects dust, dirt, and road salt, and turns into a sticky gummy abrasive paste that wears out bearings within months. Acme uses 100% industrial high-viscosity silicone spray designed specifically for sub-zero Canadian temperatures.
